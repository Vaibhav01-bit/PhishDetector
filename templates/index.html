Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about protecting your data and staying safe from phishing attacks.

What is phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where the attacker tricks the target into disclosing personal information, revealing login credentials, or transferring money. It involves someone posing as a legitimate institution to lure individuals into providing sensitive data.

Why is phishing knowledge vital?

Phishing is the #1 vector for data breaches. Understanding it prevents financial loss, protects your personal identity, and secures organizational networks from unauthorized access. A phisher's success is contingent upon establishing false trust.

What are the major types?

Deceptive Phishing: Fraudsters impersonate legitimate companies to steal data.
Spear Phishing: Targets specific individuals with customized research.
Whaling: Targets high-level executives with sophisticated social engineering.

How can individuals protect themselves?

Never give away information in unsolicited calls or emails. Inspect messages for typos, verify urgency through official channels, and use multi-layer authentication. Always verify the actual URL before clicking any link.

How do companies defend?

Invest in security awareness training to turn employees into human firewalls. Keep all software/antivirus updated and research zero-day threats regularly to maintain a vigilant security posture against evolving tactics.
