Use Case Scenarios of Phishing attack

Real-world examples of how phishing leads to massive data breaches

John Podesta

Political Campaign Hack

During the 2016 election, John Podesta’s Gmail account was compromised by a hacker group known as Fancy Bear. They sent a highly convincing fake Google security alert requesting an urgent password change.

Clicking the malicious link gave attackers total access, leading to the leak of thousands of highly confidential emails via WikiLeaks.

BenefitMall

Enterprise Credentials

Between June and October 2018, employee email login credentials were exposed via a targeted spear-phishing attack. Access to these mailboxes leaked sensitive consumer data:

  • Names and Birth Dates
  • Bank account details
  • Insurance premiums info

Sony Pictures

State-Sponsored Hack

In November 2014, a government-backed hacker group launched a devastating attack using spear-phishing emails containing malware payloads.

Infiltrators gained deep network access, stole confidential data, disabled thousands of systems, and caused over $100 million in estimated damages.

Methodist Hospitals

Healthcare Data Breach

In August 2019, an investigation revealed a phishing attack compromised more than 68,000 patients’ information after employee email accounts were breached.

Obtained data included Social Security numbers, passport numbers, medical treatment info, and electronic signatures.

UW-Parkside

Financial Wire Fraud

In June 2019, an employee was targeted by a sophisticated phishing campaign prompting them to change the routing numbers of two university system vendors.

The university lost $315,000 in fraudulent, untraceable bank transfers before discovering the breach.

Quick Phishing Guide

4 common tricks attackers use to deceive you.

Typosquatting

Fake domains with slight misspellings:

paypa1.com
instead of
paypal.com

Fake HTTPS

Phishing sites can have valid SSL certificates. HTTPS doesn't mean safe!

HTTPS ≠ Safe

Urgency

"Account suspended!" Legitimate companies don't create panic.

Take your time. Verify independently.

Spoofed Sender

Display names can be faked. Check the actual email address.

support@fake.com
